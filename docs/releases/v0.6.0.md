# CostCrew 0.6.0

A cost decision the crew recommends can now be announced before it lands, and from
its first day the anomaly queue and the forecast both read it. An optimisation that
lands as announced is no longer raised as an anomaly; one that lands short, late or
in the wrong direction is, with the decision's name on it. Only the owner can
confirm an announcement. Read the upgrade notes first.

## What changed (costcrew#121 to #127)

**Announced decisions (#127, invariant 97)**

- **A new decision class, `driver.announced`.** It names a service, a window, the
  expected change of the service's daily cost (negative for a saving), and
  optionally one team. The optimizer, optimizer-onprem, ai-spend-analyst and
  forecaster roles may propose it; only the owner's answer applies it. No role
  decides it alone, and the apply path checks that again.
- **The detector judges the step instead of the trend.** For a day inside an
  announced window, the history before the start is moved by the announced amount
  and the day is judged by the rule the queue already uses. A day that matches is
  not raised. A day that misses is raised with the decision's name, the day's
  amount and the level the decision implied. A real spike inside the window is still
  raised, and a fall that comes up to seven days early keeps its finding and gains
  the decision's name as a hint.
- **A service shared by several teams.** A service-level step is split across the
  desk's teams in proportion to each team's spend before the start (its median over
  28 days), in whole cents that add up to the step. A decision that names a team
  moves that team alone. The decision page shows the split.
- **A saving cannot exceed what it saves on.** A saving larger than the median
  daily spend of the service (or of the named team) is refused when it is proposed
  and again when it is applied, with the amount, the median and the window in the
  reason. An increase is not bounded. For a start beyond the latest data, the
  window is the 28 days up to the latest landed day.
- **The forecast carries the step before it lands.** A window that has not landed
  yet is its own line in the month's projection; once a day of it lands, the
  measured rate takes over. A frozen forecast names the announced decisions it knew.
- **Announced against measured.** After the month closes, each decision shows what
  was announced and what was measured, and how many days before or after its start
  the owner answered. When a month misses, the decision furthest from its
  announcement is named first.

**Prices**

- **The Anthropic rows are the vendor's list rates (#125, invariant 96).** The
  table the console reserves with held Claude Sonnet 5 at 3.00 / 15.00 and Claude
  Opus 5 at 15.00 / 75.00 per million tokens, both marked unverified. Anthropic's
  pricing page, read 2026-10-13, lists 2.00 / 10.00 and 5.00 / 25.00, and both rows
  now carry those rates. The settlement replay keeps the old row, because it
  replays the day it describes.

**Housekeeping**

- The Go toolchain moved to go1.27.2 (#121).
- The estate's secret scan runs on every pull request and push to main (#122).

## Upgrade notes

- **Two new columns on `drivers`, added at start:** `expected_daily_cents` and
  `team`. Rows written before this release read NULL and behave exactly as before.
  No data is rewritten.
- **One new decision class, nothing to configure.** The roles are built into the
  binary, so the four proposing roles carry `driver.announced` from the first start.
  A decision request now shows it to the owner like any other carried option.
- **No flag was added or removed** on the console or on `costcrew-run`.

## Not proven in this release

- Detection quality and forecast accuracy on real spend: every run here used the
  generated estate. On it, announcing what was actually measured raised no finding
  on the four team series of the service, and announcing nearly three times the measured
  saving raised four, each named after the decision.
- The seven-day early hint is a guess until a month of real decisions is read.
- The split uses every day before the start, while the detector judges weekdays and
  weekends separately, so a team's weekend level can sit off its share.
