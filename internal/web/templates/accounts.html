Add an account

Removing an account ends its sessions and takes it off this list. It does NOT remove anything the account did: the journal is a hash chain, and an account being deleted does not unhappen the decisions it made. The last admin cannot be removed, and neither can the one you are signed in as.

A new account starts where you put it. Nothing is promoted automatically, and a joining code cannot hand out power.
